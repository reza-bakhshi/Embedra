not implicitly covered by the verified generic chip-ID rows. Every T026 record
begins `PLANNED` until its own provider, topology, fixtures, and acceptance
evidence exist. STM32 source-reference records are described below. Raspberry Pi
silicon records (RP2040, RP2350A/B, RP2354A/B), board profiles (Pico, Pico W, Pico 2, Pico 2 W),
UF2 container parsing, and freestanding fixtures are described in the Raspberry Pi section below.
