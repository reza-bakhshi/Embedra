# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Members of the Embedra community — including maintainers, contributors, and
anyone engaging with the project by reporting issues, security findings, or
firmware evidence — pledge to make participation a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our
  mistakes, and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the
  overall community;
- keeping evidence discussions factual: firmware analysis involves legal
  zones around redistributable material, so private captures, unlicensed
  vendor data, and secrets are handled exactly as `docs/fixture-policy.md`
  and `SECURITY.md` prescribe.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any
  kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission;
- distributing malware, exploit payloads, or stolen credentials to the
  community under the guise of "test material" without the documented
  fixture-provenance process;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards
of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action
in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not
aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation
decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (issues, pull
requests, discussions, releases) and also applies when an individual is
officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples of
representing our community include maintaining the repository and acting as
a designated contact in security or vendor discussions.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the community leaders responsibly — use GitHub private
vulnerability/contact channels described in `SECURITY.md` and
`CONTRIBUTING.md`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly
and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of
the reporter of any incident.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the general contributor-community
standards used by well-known open-source projects, written for the concrete
context of this repository. It may evolve with reviewed specification
changes, as with all repository documentation.
