## Corrections

1. Original ESP32 and ESP32-S2 imports no longer write misleading
   `ESP32S3_ENRICHMENT_*` diagnostics or the ESP32-S3 import option namespace.
   They now use the family-neutral `Embedra ESP Import` namespace with
   `ESP32_ENRICHMENT_*` and `ESP32S2_ENRICHMENT_*` codes. ESP32-S3 retains its
   established namespace and codes for compatibility.
2. `validate-handoff.sh` now requires safe evidence for every completed task,
   unique completed-task identifiers, agreement between a complete current
   task and its primary evidence, an unchecked next task in the specification,
   and safe evidence for every acceptance gate. A negative policy test proves
   that missing historical task evidence is rejected.

The stale intermediate test count and the contradictory catalog wording in
`PROJECT_STATE.md` were also corrected.
