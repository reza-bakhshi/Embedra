## Limitations and next action

These fixtures prove format/oracle properties only. They are not hardware boot tests and do not verify an exact package, embedded-memory variant, detector, parser, provider, loader, or device-support state. The `fixture-no-idf-runtime` value deliberately prevents the synthetic freestanding image from masquerading as an ESP-IDF application. No malformed fixture exists yet.
