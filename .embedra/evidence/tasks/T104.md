## 3. Boundary & Invariant Summary

- **Series**: 5 (`ATmega`, `ATtiny`, `AVR-Dx`, `SAMD`, `SAME`).
- **Exact Parts**: 27 (7 ATmega, 6 ATtiny, 5 AVR-Dx, 5 SAMD, 4 SAME/SAM3).
- **Mandatory Parts Audited**: `ATmega328P`, `ATmega2560`, `ATmega32U4`, `ATmega128`,
  `ATtiny85`, `ATtiny13A`, `ATtiny84`, `ATtiny1614`, `AVR128DA48`, `AVR128DB48`,
  `SAMD21G18A`, `SAMD51J19A`, `SAME70Q21`, `SAM3X8E`.
- **Support State**: All 27 parts initialize as `PLANNED` per ATMEL-010.
- **Physical Memory Anchors**:
  - AVR Flash (`code`): `0x00000000` (word-addressed PC semantics)
  - AVR SRAM (`data`): Starts at `0x0060`, `0x0100`, or `0x0200` (following R0-R31 and I/O registers)
  - AVR EEPROM (`eeprom`): Starts at `0x0000`
  - AVR I/O (`io`): 64 standard I/O registers at `0x00`..`0x3F`
  - AVR-Dx Unified Data Space: Flash alias window at `0x8000`, SRAM at `0x4000`, EEPROM at `0x1400`
  - SAM Flash: `0x00000000` (SAMD), `0x00400000` (SAME70), `0x00080000` (SAM3X)
  - SAM SRAM: `0x20000000` (SAMD/SAM3X), `0x20400000` (SAME70)
  - SAM Peripherals: `0x40000000`
- **Vector Stride**:
  - 2 bytes (`RJMP`) for classic AVR parts with $\le$ 8 KiB Flash (`ATtiny85`, `ATtiny13A`, `ATmega8A`, etc.)
  - 4 bytes (`JMP` / Thumb) for classic AVR > 8 KiB, AVR-Dx, and SAM Cortex-M parts.
- **Matching Policy**: Exact part string match only; wildcards and family prefixes rejected.
