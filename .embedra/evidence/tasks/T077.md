## 4. Design decisions and provenance

- Every capability statement was cross-checked against
  `docs/support-matrix.md`, `docs/compatibility-matrix.md`, `.embedra/state.json`,
  the T065/T066 evidence, and the source tree (analyzer/loader names, option
  categories, diagnostic codes, catalog digests). The earlier README's
  Nordic and STM8 "supported target" sections contradicted SpecKit §1.4
  (planned provider advertised as working) and were replaced with an honest
  planned-scope table pointing at T088–T111.
- `analyzeHeadless` flags were transcribed from the pinned Ghidra 12.1.3
  `support/analyzeHeadlessREADME.md` shipped in the local Ghidra
  distribution, not from memory.
- The link checker enforces only structural facts (existence, relative
  resolution, anchors, forbidden host-path markers) to avoid becoming a
  prose-lint gate.

## 5. Limitations

- The shipped user-operation documents describe only implemented behavior;
  Gtk-based help integration, PDF builds, and screenshot imagery are not
  shipped and remain unclaimed until the T079 release-bundle convergence
  produces them from working builds.
- `docs/agent-handoff.md` documents the continuity contract but cannot
  enforce prose quality; the checker validates structure only.
- No new device roles, pack content, or support-state changes were made:
  T077 is documentation and enforcement only.
