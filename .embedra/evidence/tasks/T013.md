## Implementation

- A bundled tabular chip-ID registry records ESP32-S3 chip ID 9 and source
  provenance. Its narrow T013 scope is explicit; T026 remains responsible for
  complete generated Espressif catalog coverage.
- `Esp32S3TargetProvider` supports only images whose parsed chip ID matches the
  maintained ESP32-S3 definition.
- Language selection is `Xtensa:LE:32:default`, compiler `default`, 32-bit,
  little-endian, with one-byte instruction alignment from the pinned Ghidra
  language definition.
- Range classification requires a complete half-open range inside one ESP-IDF
  DROM, IROM, IRAM, or DRAM region and the `default` address space. Crossing,
  empty, and foreign-space ranges are not mappable.
- Proposed permissions are DROM `R`, DRAM `RW`, and IROM/IRAM image segments
  `RX`.
- Entry decisions require selected-language alignment, recognized executable
  memory, containment in an encoded image segment, and executable
  classification of that complete segment.
- All decisions retain evidence and remain independent of Ghidra APIs.
