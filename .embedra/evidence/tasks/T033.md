# T033 Evidence — STM32 golden fixtures and selected-part validation

## Status

`COMPLETE` on 2026-09-30. T032's reviewed 53-part exact allowlist is the
input. T034 and STM32 firmware import have not started.

## First fixture slice

- A failing Python test first demonstrated the missing T033 validator.
- Repository-authored freestanding Cortex-M3 startup assembly and linker
  template generate ELF32 ARM, Intel HEX, and raw BIN goldens for
  STM32F103C8T6, STM32F103CBT6, and STM32F103RBT6. The generator performs
  two isolated builds and compares every artifact and oracle byte for byte.
- Readelf header/program-header oracles, exact-target manifests, generator
  source digest, toolchain identity, and artifact SHA-256 values are retained.
  The vector encodes stack `0x20005000` and Thumb reset `0x08000101`.
- The independent validator checks ELF machine/entry, Intel HEX record
  checksums and addressed bytes, raw vector/text, exact fixture set, source
  binding, and F103 catalog package/flash/RAM facts. It checks all 53 selected
  catalog records for exact identity, sourced/ambiguous memory, processor and
  IRQ structure, provenance digests, and explicit unknown facts. Mutation
  tests reject duplicate IDs, unsupported state promotion, fabricated memory
  pairing, unsafe IRQ promotion, and malformed processor/IRQ entries.

## Second fixture slice

STM32C051C8T6, STM32C071RBT6, and STM32C092RCT6 now have independently
generated Cortex-M0+ ELF/HEX/BIN sets. Their exact catalog values are
64/12, 128/24, and 256/30 KiB flash/RAM respectively, with three distinct
package identities and concrete IRQ mappings. ST's Apache-2.0 CMSIS device
headers from STM32Cube FW C0 V1.4.0 attest `FLASH_BASE=0x08000000` and
`SRAM_BASE=0x20000000`; the per-header digests are retained in the oracles.
The ST linker template was inspected for a cross-check but not copied.
Original assembly sets the part-specific stack top and Thumb reset
`0x08000101`. The generator compares two isolated builds per part, retains
readelf and per-artifact hashes, and copies no ST source into the fixtures.
The validator checks all three formats and binds each to its exact selected
record and source provenance. A negative test corrupts C071 HEX data and
confirms rejection.

## M4/M7/M33 fixture slice

Three more exact selected parts have original ELF/HEX/BIN samples:
STM32L412KBU3 (M4), STM32F756ZGT6 (M7), and STM32H503RBT6 (M33). The
independent pinned ST CMSIS header hashes equal the catalog's recorded header
hashes. Their authoritative addresses establish distinct safe stack banks:
L412 SRAM1 32 KiB of 40 KiB total (`0x20008000` stack top), F756 DTCM 64
KiB of 320 KiB total (`0x20010000`), and H503 nonsecure SRAM1 16 KiB of 32
KiB total (`0x20004000`). The original minimal reset vector is `0x08000101`.
The fixture generator compares two isolated builds, and the validator checks
ELF, HEX, BIN, exact catalog identity, source revision/hash, and package facts.
The source files are not copied. This sample does not prove TrustZone,
multi-bank mapping, or hardware behavior.

## M55 RAM sample

STM32N657X0H3Q now has a fourth core-sample fixture for its Cortex-M55 and
zero-internal-flash case. The pinned Apache-2.0 ST N6 CMSIS header independently
attests secure SRAM1_AXI base `0x34000000` and size 1 MiB. ELF and Intel HEX
vectors are RAM addressed; the raw BIN requires an explicit SRAM base. The
stack is `0x34100000`, and the Thumb entry is `0x34000101`. Its selected
catalog still has conditional interrupt mapping, so the sample does not add
IRQ or board-boot claims. The source generator produces two byte-identical
builds, and the validator checks all three formats plus exact catalog identity
and source provenance.

## Final fixture coverage and boundary

Fourteen more exact samples cover F2, F4, G0, G4, H7, L4+, L5, U0, U3, U5,
WB0, WB, WBA, and WL3. Their pinned Apache-2.0 ST CMSIS header SHA-256
values match the selected catalog's recorded hashes, and each retained oracle
names the address macros checked. ELF/HEX/BIN artifacts regenerate identically
in two isolated builds. F2, F4, G0, G4, and L4+ use sourced SRAM vectors
because their exact flash sizes remain `SOURCE_UNPAIRED`. WB0 also uses a
sourced SRAM vector; its `FLASH_BASE` macro denotes the peripheral register
block. WL3's main flash vector is at `0x10040000`, preserving its atypical
address. Stack regions use a sourced bank or conservative window rather than
the aggregate RAM value.

The 24 fixture sets now cover all 20 selected family labels and the selected
M0+/M3/M4/M7/M33/M55 processor types. All 53 selected identities have
structural validation. The other 29 parts remain catalog only without a golden.
These are deterministic synthetic firmware, not vendor firmware or evidence
that input bytes identify an exact part. The T033 fixture criterion is met by
representative goldens for all selected family labels and all selected core
types, with structural validation for every exact selected record. The 29
unfixtured identities and every selected target remain `PLANNED`; no selected
STM32 part gains import support from T033.
