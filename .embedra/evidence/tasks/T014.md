## Test-first contract

`EspApplicationImageMapperTest` uses a real initialized Ghidra `ProgramDB` and
requires T015 to prove:

- all three segment byte arrays are mapped exactly at their encoded DROM,
  DRAM, and IRAM addresses;
- block ends, sizes, names, and permissions are deterministic (`R`, `RW`,
  `RX` respectively);
- the validated image entry becomes an external entry point with deterministic
  `entry` symbol;
- the import report records commit status, exact device/language/compiler,
  three segments, and entry address;
- a pre-existing user DRAM block triggers a precise mapping failure, remains
  byte-for-byte and permission-for-permission unchanged, and leaves no partial
  DROM/IRAM blocks or entry point;
- closing the existing transaction wrapper without commit rolls back a real
  ProgramDB memory mutation.
