# T100 Evidence — Nordic SVD / MDK Peripheral Register Data Packs & CryptoCell Definitions

## Status

`COMPLETE` on 2026-10-14. T100 implements authoritative Nordic Semiconductor peripheral register
definitions, EasyDMA schemas, ARM TrustZone SPU/DPPI registers, and hardware cryptographic accelerators
(CryptoCell CC310 and CC312) across all 5 Nordic series: nRF51, nRF52, nRF53, nRF54, and nRF91.
The peripheral register packs enforce 32-bit word alignment, strict MMIO aperture bounds
(`0x40000000..0x5FFFFFFF`), non-overlapping bitfields, bounded `ParserLimits`, cooperative cancellation,
and SHA-256 integrity verification against schema `"urn:embedra:schema:nordic-peripheral-pack:1"`.
Comprehensive unit tests validate peripheral structures, EasyDMA memory apertures, CryptoCell block
definitions, loader limits, cancellation, and checksum verification in accordance with `Embedra-SpecKit.md`
(T100, NRF-004, NRF-008, NRF-009, NRF-010, Section 8.3A, and Section 12).

## 1. Deliverables

1. **Peripheral Pack Domain Model**:
   - `src/main/java/io/github/rezabakhshi/embedra/targets/nordic/peripheral/NordicBitField.java`:
     - 32-bit register bitfield record validating bit offsets (`0..31`), bit widths (`1..32`),
       contiguous bitmasks, access modes, and single-bit factory helper.
   - `src/main/java/io/github/rezabakhshi/embedra/targets/nordic/peripheral/NordicEasyDmaType.java`:
     - Enumeration capturing EasyDMA register roles: `PTR`, `MAXCNT`, `AMOUNT`, `LIST`.
   - `src/main/java/io/github/rezabakhshi/embedra/targets/nordic/peripheral/NordicEasyDmaValidator.java`:
     - Enforces 32-bit word alignment on DMA pointers, aperture access rules (SRAM `0x20000000..0x2FFFFFFF`,
       read-only Flash/QSPI), and strict rejection of MMIO (`0x40000000..0x5FFFFFFF`) and FICR/UICR apertures.
   - `src/main/java/io/github/rezabakhshi/embedra/targets/nordic/peripheral/NordicRegister.java`:
     - Word-aligned register record enforcing MMIO apertures, 4-byte alignment, non-overlapping bitfields,
       and EasyDMA flags.
   - `src/main/java/io/github/rezabakhshi/embedra/targets/nordic/peripheral/NordicPeripheral.java`:
     - Hardware peripheral block validating aperture bounds, alignment, duplicate addresses/names, register lookups,
       and EasyDMA register filtering.
   - `src/main/java/io/github/rezabakhshi/embedra/targets/nordic/peripheral/NordicPeripheralPack.java`:
     - Immutable peripheral pack containing all peripherals for a Nordic series, enforcing non-overlapping blocks.
   - `src/main/java/io/github/rezabakhshi/embedra/targets/nordic/peripheral/NordicPeripheralSchemaTransform.java`:
     - Transforms register definitions into structured Ghidra EOL and PRE comments with EasyDMA details,
       and formats peripheral PLATE comments.

2. **Loader, Registry & Cryptographic Definitions**:
   - `src/main/java/io/github/rezabakhshi/embedra/targets/nordic/peripheral/NordicPeripheralPackLoader.java`:
     - Strict bounded JSON loader with `ParserLimits`, `CancellationToken`, and SHA-256 integrity verification.
   - `src/main/java/io/github/rezabakhshi/embedra/targets/nordic/peripheral/NordicCryptoCellDefinition.java`:
     - Authoritative hardware definitions for ARM TrustZone CryptoCell CC310 (nRF52840 at `0x5002A000`, 4 KiB)
       and CC312 (nRF5340 / nRF9160 at `0x50840000`, 4 KiB), covering Host Control, AES, Hash, PKA, and TRNG.
   - `src/main/java/io/github/rezabakhshi/embedra/targets/nordic/peripheral/NordicDefaultPacks.java`:
     - Built-in peripheral packs across all 5 series covering core peripherals: NVMC, CLOCK, POWER, RADIO,
       TIMER, RTC, UART/UARTE, SPI/SPIM, TWI/TWIM, GPIOTE, WDT, RNG, TEMP, SPU, DPPI, CC310, CC312.
   - `src/main/java/io/github/rezabakhshi/embedra/targets/nordic/peripheral/NordicDevicePackRegistry.java`:
     - Pack registry loading from `catalogs/nordic/packs` with seamless fallback to `NordicDefaultPacks`.

3. **Data Packs & Generator Scripts**:
   - `scripts/generate-nordic-packs.py`:
     - Generates `catalogs/nordic/packs/{nrf51,nrf52,nrf53,nrf54,nrf91}-peripherals.json` and `.sha256`.
   - `scripts/tests/test_nordic_packs.py`:
     - Unit test verifying generated pack JSON schemas, non-overlapping registers, and SHA-256 digests.

4. **Automated Unit Tests**:
   - `src/test/java/io/github/rezabakhshi/embedra/targets/nordic/NordicPeripheralPackTest.java`:
     - Complete test suite verifying bitfields, registers, EasyDMA validation, CryptoCell blocks,
       SPU and DPPI definitions, pack loader bounds, cancellation, and checksum verification.
